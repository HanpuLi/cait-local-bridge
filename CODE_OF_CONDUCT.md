# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be usable by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be specific about code and behavior, assume good faith until evidence says otherwise, and keep review focused on the technical question.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate doxxing, sexual harassment, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Security reports must not be used to expose private operator data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct materially disrupts the project or puts people at risk.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's available private contact/security mechanisms. Do not include secrets or third-party personal data unless necessary to explain the report.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional to the behavior and should distinguish disagreement about code from abuse of people.
